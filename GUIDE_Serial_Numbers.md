# Serial numbers – step by step

Every piece of furniture or equipment can carry its serial number. The quantity stays as before; serial numbers are
optional (chairs usually have none, TV screens and fridges do).

## Add new pieces with their serial numbers
1. Open the break area → **Add New** (or *Update* in the inventory).
2. Item: for example *TV Screens*. Action: **Added**.
3. **Serial numbers**: type them, one per line – or click in the box and scan the labels with a barcode scanner.
   The quantity follows the number of lines (you can still add pieces without a serial number).
4. **Save Update**. The transaction says which serial numbers came.

## Pieces that are already there
On the break area page open **Serial numbers** (under the inventory) → **Add** (or **Edit**) next to the item, one
per line, **Save Serial Numbers**. You can also correct a typing mistake here. There cannot be more serial numbers
than pieces.

## Remove or transfer pieces
Choose **Removed** or **Transferred**: tick the serial numbers of the pieces that go. When the remaining pieces would
be fewer than the recorded serial numbers, the program asks which ones go. Transferred pieces move with their serial
number to the other break area.

## Exchange a piece
Choose **Replaced**, pick the **old serial number** and type the **new serial number**.

## Find a serial number
**Furniture & Equipment → Serial Numbers**: type part of the number, the break area or the item. Click a row to open
the break area. The same serial number cannot be entered twice; if it happens on two PCs at the same time, the list
marks it with **twice**.

## Reports
*Reports → Inventory by Break Area* and the *Full Excel Export* (sheet *Serial Numbers*) show them.
