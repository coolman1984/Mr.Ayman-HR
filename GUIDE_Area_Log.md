# Area Log and maintenance – step by step

## Write down what happens in a break area
Break area page → **Area Log** → **Add Note**. Choose the type (Note, Painting, Renovation, Repair, Cleaning, Replacement, Other), the date and
write what happened. Notes can be changed by their writer and by people who may edit break areas; only those people can delete them.
The log shows notes together with maintenance, issues (reported and closed), inspections and Before / After photos, newest first.
The buttons in the log print it or save it as Excel. **Reports → Area History** does the same for one or all break areas.

## The work is already finished
**Record Finished Work**: type, date, item (and the serial number of the piece), who did it, what was done, contractor, warranty date and – for people who may see
costs – the cost. **Plan the same work again** plans the next one (for example painting every 2 years). If the work solved an open issue, choose it: the issue is closed
together with the work.

## Plan work in advance
**Schedule Maintenance**: type, item / piece, date, who, contractor, related issue, **Repeat** (never, every month, 3 months, 6 months, year, 2 years).
Late or due-within-a-week work is marked on the Maintenance page and counted at the bell. **Complete** it when done (contractor, cost, warranty, close the related issue).
Repeating work plans its next date by itself when completed.

## Cancel instead of delete
The small **×** → reason → **Cancel the work**. It stays in the history as Cancelled. (The trash can still deletes for good, like before.)

## Photos of a piece of work
Upload photos and choose **Belongs to this work** (categories Before / After). Open the work with the small page symbol in the Area Log: its photos are shown together.

## Costs
The permission **"See and enter what maintenance work cost"** (Users & Permissions) decides who sees costs. Administrators always have it. Without it there are no costs
on screens, reports, the change log or Excel, and saving a work cannot change or erase them.

## History of one piece
Break area page → **Serial numbers** → click a serial number: all repairs, movements and planned work of that piece. Three repairs or more: *think about replacing it*.

## Future plans
**Future Plans** (under the log): *Add Plan* – what, target date (optional), priority. **Schedule** plans the work with the text filled in; completing that work marks the plan done.
The ✓ marks a plan done by hand, the × drops it (it stays in the list). Plans past their date are on the dashboard (*Needs Attention*) and the Maintenance page shows what is coming.

## Font and text size
**Aa** at the top (or Settings → Appearance): six fonts and six text sizes, for you only (kept on this PC and browser, no internet needed).

## Import, search, icons
**Break Areas → Import from Excel** (template to download; nothing that exists is changed, so importing twice is harmless), **Ctrl K** searches everything, item types get an icon
from more than 600 icons in the item type window (typing the name suggests one), **Settings → Data Safety** shows what the last update did to your data and can check it at any time.
